The glossary has a page for everything the capabilities library provides, in two parts:

- [Video cards](/docs/capabilities-glossary/video-cards/): the list of cards, the card, its vendor, and the graphics preference that picks one, read and set. In the sidebar each type opens to its members.
- [Displays](/docs/capabilities-glossary/displays/): the list of displays, the display, its modes and resolutions, the desktop's rectangles and how a display is turned. In the sidebar each type opens to its members.
