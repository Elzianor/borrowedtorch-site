The glossary has a page for everything the shaping functions library provides, in three parts:

- [Functions](/docs/shaping-functions-glossary/functions/): the 44 curves, each with a picture, its formula and its parameters. In the sidebar they are grouped by category.
- [Types](/docs/shaping-functions-glossary/types/): the base class of every function, the factory, the catalog and the two enums that name the functions and their categories. In the sidebar each type opens to its members.
- [Parameters](/docs/shaping-functions-glossary/parameters/): the interfaces that give a function its parameters A to D and its order N. In the sidebar each interface opens to its property.
