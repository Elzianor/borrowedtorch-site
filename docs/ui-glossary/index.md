The glossary has a page for everything the UI provides, in two parts:

- [Elements](/docs/ui-glossary/elements/): the base element and every concrete element, with their properties, methods and events. In the sidebar each element opens to its members.
- [Types](/docs/ui-glossary/types/): every other type: classes such as the styles and the animations, structs, interfaces, enums with their values, the delegates of the events, and the types of the [markup](/docs/ui-markup/) package.
