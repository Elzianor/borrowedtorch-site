Each type has a page listing its members in alphabetical order, each with its type or signature and what it does. The sidebar lists the members of each type under its name.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [`Fabric`](/docs/fabric-simulation-glossary/sheet/fabric/) | The sheet: builds the grid, keeps its pins and its damage, cuts it and drives the solver every frame. |
| [`FabricSettings`](/docs/fabric-simulation-glossary/sheet/fabric-settings/) | Every tunable of the solver and the sheet: the grid and spacing, the solver, bending, the air, the colliders, self-collision and rest. |
| [`FabricOrientation`](/docs/fabric-simulation-glossary/sheet/fabric-orientation/) | The plane a sheet is built in: vertical or horizontal. |
| [`FabricPinMode`](/docs/fabric-simulation-glossary/sheet/fabric-pin-mode/) | The preset layouts of pins: none, the top edge, the top corners, the left edge or the four corners. |
