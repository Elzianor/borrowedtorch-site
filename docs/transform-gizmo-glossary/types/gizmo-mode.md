Which handles the gizmo shows.

An enum, the type of <a class="ref prop" href="/docs/transform-gizmo-glossary/gizmo/transform-gizmo/#mode">`Mode`</a>. The [modes and frames page](/docs/transform-gizmo-modes/#modes) shows it in context.

## Values

<h3 id="translate"><code>Translate</code> <a class="ctx" href="/docs/transform-gizmo-modes/#gizmomode-values" title="Translate in context on the modes and frames page" aria-label="Translate in context on the modes and frames page">→</a></h3>

Three arrowed arms, each moving the target along its axis, and three plane squares, each moving it across its plane. The default.

<h3 id="rotate"><code>Rotate</code> <a class="ctx" href="/docs/transform-gizmo-modes/#gizmomode-values" title="Rotate in context on the modes and frames page" aria-label="Rotate in context on the modes and frames page">→</a></h3>

Three rings, each turning the target about its axis.

<h3 id="scale"><code>Scale</code> <a class="ctx" href="/docs/transform-gizmo-modes/#gizmomode-values" title="Scale in context on the modes and frames page" aria-label="Scale in context on the modes and frames page">→</a></h3>

Three arms tipped with boxes, each scaling one of the target's axes, three plane squares, each scaling two, and the center square, scaling all three.
