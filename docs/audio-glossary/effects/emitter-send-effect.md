The start of every emitter's chain: the samples of the current clip.

`EmitterSendEffect` derives from [`BaseAudioEffect`](/docs/audio-glossary/effects/base-audio-effect/) and has its members, with none of its own. The [effect chains page](/docs/audio-effects/#emittersendeffect-and-emitterreturneffect) shows it in context, with code.
