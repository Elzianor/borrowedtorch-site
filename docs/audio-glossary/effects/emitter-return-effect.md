The end of every emitter's chain: what reaches it goes to the mix.

`EmitterReturnEffect` derives from [`BaseAudioEffect`](/docs/audio-glossary/effects/base-audio-effect/) and has its members, with none of its own. The [effect chains page](/docs/audio-effects/#emittersendeffect-and-emitterreturneffect) shows it in context, with code.
