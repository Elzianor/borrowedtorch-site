The glossary has a page for everything the audio library provides, in two parts:

- [Effects](/docs/audio-glossary/effects/): the base effect and every effect, with their properties and methods. In the sidebar each effect opens to its members.
- [Types](/docs/audio-glossary/types/): every other type: the manager, the emitter interface, the device notification client, the enums with their values, and the delegates of the events.
