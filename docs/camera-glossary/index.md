The glossary has a page for everything the camera library provides, in three parts:

- [Cameras and controllers](/docs/camera-glossary/cameras/): the camera, the base of every controller, and the animated and free controllers. In the sidebar each type opens to its members.
- [Paths](/docs/camera-glossary/paths/): the camera path, its waypoints and samples, and the types that draw it. In the sidebar each type opens to its members.
- [Types](/docs/camera-glossary/types/): the enums, with their values.
