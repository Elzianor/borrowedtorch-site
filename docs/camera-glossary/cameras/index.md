Each type has a page listing its members in alphabetical order, each with its type or signature and what it does. The sidebar lists the members of each type under its name.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [`Camera`](/docs/camera-glossary/cameras/camera/) | A position and an orientation, the view and projection matrices, picking rays and a floating origin. |
| [`BaseCameraController`](/docs/camera-glossary/cameras/base-camera-controller/) | The abstract base of every controller, for controllers of your own. |
| [`AnimatedCameraController`](/docs/camera-glossary/cameras/animated-camera-controller/) | Plays a camera path at a cruise speed with its waypoints' easing and holds, with play, pause, seek and an eased entry. |
| [`FreeCameraController`](/docs/camera-glossary/cameras/free-camera-controller/) | Flight with six degrees of freedom, from the player's input. |
