The modifier a key binding is held with.

An enum, in the namespace `BorrowedTorch.InputBinding.Types`. The <a class="ref prop" href="/docs/input-binder-glossary/inputs/button-input/#modifier">`Modifier`</a> of a button input, and part of its identity: S and Ctrl+S are two inputs. Only keys carry one; a binding has one at most. The [modifier keys page](/docs/input-binder-modifiers/#buttonmodifier) shows it in context.
